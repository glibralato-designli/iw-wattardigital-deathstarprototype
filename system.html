Foundations

A navy frame holds white work. Navy lives in the shell; inside the panels the page is light, flat and bordered. Every colour is a semantic role with a light and a dark value.

Shell and panels

The sidebar and top bar are one navy frame (--shell). Content sits in white panels with a 12px radius and an 8px navy gutter. Nothing filled navy touches the frame directly.

Typography

Inter, one family, Attio-like scale: 14px is the default UI size. Weights 400, 500 and 600 (700 only for the predictive score and confidence). Sentence case everywhere; no capitals, no letter-spaced labels. Numbers use tabular figures.

Badges and chips

Class badges rank sellers (A navy, B accent, C outline, D muted, LH dashed). Trust badges carry certainty. Chips hold metadata and filters.

Tabs

Page tabs sit under the title with a 2px navy underline, the only single-side border in the system. A divider separates groups; disabled tabs are drawn but greyed.
