# Death Star — POC prototype

## Purpose
A redesign proposal for the Stewart Group's internal commercial real-estate prospecting portal (client: Wattar Digital). The portal tells a broker who is most likely to sell, and why now. The POC proves that a calm navy shell, with Today as the one place the day starts, lets the broker reach the right owner, judge whether the why-now is safe to say out loud, and log the call in seconds.

**The question every screen answers:** who is most likely to sell, and why now?

**Core loop:** Today (greeting, meetings this week, the ranked list) → open the first owner → read the why now and its trust → Call → log the outcome → Next owner.

## Audience
**Thomas Windels** (fictional demo persona), NYC multifamily broker at Stewart Group. His territory is **Main Body Manhattan, from 100th St to the southern tip** (walkthrough P3 01:55); the Lower East Side is where most of the demo's owners sit. Non-technical, on the phone all day, used to Salesforce-style horizontal tabs. Wants "classic corporate", midnight blue, quiet, not data-dense. His fear: saying something wrong to an owner. Secondary: Mira W., the builder at Wattar Digital, and Giulio Libralato (Designli), who presents the proposal.

## Success test (first client call, 1440 × 900, no guidance)
1. From Today (the first page), Thomas reaches the first owner to call in 1 click and ≤ 10 seconds, without scrolling.
2. After one glance at the why-now card, he says whether it's safe to say out loud.
3. He logs a no-answer call in 2 clicks after ending it.
4. He finds any area from the sidebar on the first try, and names the 7 Dashboard tabs.
5. He reacts to the shell (navy frame, grouped sidebar, Today as the start, Dashboard › Overview tiles) as a structure: keep, change or reject.

## Experience principles
1. **Summary first, detail on request.** Every page leads with a sentence or a number, never a table. Tables live inside a tab or after a click; a section shows about 6 items, then "View all".
2. **Trust before persuasion.** A why now never looks more certain than its evidence. Every claim carries its trust status: Verified · Single source · Stale · Disputed, always colour + word + icon. Single-source facts read "public records suggest". A disputed fact outside the why now shows a warning that names it ("Mailing address is disputed. It isn’t part of this why now."); flagging a fact as wrong turns the why now Disputed with "Check before you say it."
3. **One of everything.** One why now, one score per building, one rank per person, one assistant (Brain), one place per destination. Keep Thomas's labels and order; change the structure, not the vocabulary.

## Tone
Plain, broker-to-broker. One real figure per sentence, no hype, no exclamation marks. Sentence case. Missing data reads "Not on record".

## Platform
web

Responsive web, designed at 1440 × 900 and fluid from 1920 down to a 390 phone. Desktop: labelled sidebar, Brain minimized beside the page (docked when opened). Below about 1360 Brain overlays the page ; below about 1180 the sidebar is the icon rail. Tablet (under 1024): records and the Dashboard's right columns stack under the main column, the search and Links collapse to icons, tables scroll sideways inside their card. Phone (under 768): the sidebar is a drawer behind a menu button, the top bar shows the current page, Brain, the peek and dialogs take the whole screen, grids become one column, the calendar opens on Day, and fields use 16px text. Brain opened on a phone stays on that screen only. On the Brain page the chat history is a column that can be hidden on wider screens, and a left drawer behind a Chats button on a phone.

- **Top bar** (navy): breadcrumb, search (⌘K), Links menu, notifications (disabled), Ask Brain toggle (⌘J).
- **Page tabs** under each page title. Every destination appears once; views are tabs, never sidebar items.
- **Brain panel**: docked right inside the frame. Minimized on every page until opened, hidden on the Brain page.

**Freshness on screen** (3.0 · 1.6; Orientation doc 3, doc 1 §8). Lead owner Daniel Marchetti, Ludlow 118 Holdings LLC, 118 Ludlow St, $9.375M loan due June 9, 2026. 15 demo contacts and 17 properties: most on the Lower East Side and in the East Village, plus Erik Lindqvist (311 E 82nd St, Upper East Side), Kwame Osei (418 W 46th St, Midtown West), Claire Brennan (236 W 22nd St, Chelsea) and one owner outside the territory, Marc Dubois (130 W 130th St, Central Harlem). Counts are at the scale of Thomas's database (about 40,000 contacts, 36,480 in the territory, 18,640 properties; lists, Database totals and Reporting use these numbers), while the table shows the fictional demo rows. The Database's In territory switch (on by default) hides owners and buildings north of 100th St. The calendar holds meetings, scheduled call blocks, BOV pitches and tours only; LinkedIn posts, Constant Contact sends and mail merges are planned in the Attack Plan's week strip. Calendar events with a contact appear in that contact's Activity (past ones in the timeline, upcoming ones under Upcoming and overdue). Saving an outcome applies its results (above) and its actions: Mark number primary moves the Primary badge to the number reached, Mark number bad retires it, and Do not contact, every channel opts the owner out permanently; each is recorded in History. Database search and ⌘K match name, entity, phone, email, address and BBL. Brain's composer: the paperclip opens the simulated Drop files and Learning opens Learning Mode; @, / and Ask and Act are drawn but off. Engagement per contact (campaigns, OM clicks, listing views, downloads) and LinkedIn connections drive the list criteria. Team history is fictional: John Stewart proposed on 118 Ludlow St in 2004, listed it in 2005 and sold it in 2006 to Daniel Marchetti (the current owner; the fixtures have him buying in 2006, so the dates differ from the walkthrough's 2012 example), did a BOV in 2012, and Thomas met him in 2019; Sarah Klein and David Ortiz are the other team members. Marchetti's Relationship carries notes from 2019 and 2021 in his Activity. evidence in all four trust statuses, a week of calendar events, conversations, goals and Overview metrics. "Today" is Wednesday, September 30, 2026. Logged calls, notes, flags, to-dos, events and conversations persist in browser local storage; "Reset prototype data" in the account menu (or `?reset=1`) restores the seed. Brain answers are scripted.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast in both themes; focus ring on every control (white ring on navy); landmarks and a skip link; `role="tablist"` with arrow keys; `aria-live` for toasts and streaming; keyboard ⌘K, ⌘J, `[`, Esc. Disabled controls change colour only (never opacity), stay focusable with the tooltip "Not in this proposal yet", and do nothing on click. Trust is never colour alone.
